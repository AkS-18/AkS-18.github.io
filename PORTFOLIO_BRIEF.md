## 3. Featured Hackathon Projects

### Project 1: BookByBlock
* **Domain:** Blockchain, Decentralized Ticketing & Smart Contracts
* **Overview:** BookByBlock is a blockchain-powered ticketing platform designed to make event ticketing more secure, transparent, and fair. It uses NFT-based tickets, dynamic QR codes, and blockchain verification to prevent ticket fraud, duplicate tickets, and unauthorized resale. Users can securely purchase, manage, and verify tickets, while event organizers get better control over ticket distribution and ownership.
* **Tech Stack:** Solidity, Ethereum/Web3, Dynamic QR Verification, React, Smart Contracts.

### Project 2: WeatherGPT
* **Domain:** Applied AI, Conversational Intelligence & Climate Modeling
* **Overview:** WeatherGPT is an AI-powered weather platform designed to make weather information easier to understand and act upon. It combines personalized weather insights, interactive maps, what-if weather simulations, multilingual support, and safety alerts into a single user-friendly interface. The project focuses on transforming complex weather data into clear, practical information that helps users better understand changing weather conditions and make informed decisions.
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Large Language Model APIs, Interactive Map APIs, Weather APIs, Climate Simulations.
